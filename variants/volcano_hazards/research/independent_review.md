## Is the team-made diagram scientifically fair?
Mostly yes, as a generic schematic. Each panel is labelled "Illustration, not to scale (team-made)", with a "DIAGRAM · TEAM-MADE · NOT TO SCALE" pill and the credit line "Team-made illustration". Panels: 1 seafloor shoved up, 2 whole water column rises, 3 long waves race outward. This matches NOAA ("vertical movement of the ocean floor... the water above it rises"). Weak points: it depicts only the earthquake case (see #5); wave shapes are exaggerated, which "not to scale" covers; it never shows that deep-ocean tsunami height is small (NOAA: under about 3 ft) and that wavelength is very long; and it gives no data, so no implication of measured values. It does not imply real data. No fake footage is used.

## Looks good
- Hook text in frame 0 and the first audio at 0.1 s ("Volcanoes can kill without lava.").
- Every shot carries a pill with its type ("REAL FOOTAGE", "PHOTO", "DIAGRAM", "MODEL ANIMATION", "SATELLITE OBSERVATION", "HISTORICAL ILLUSTRATION", "FACT CARD") plus a place/date, and a per-shot credit line at the bottom (USGS/HVO, USGS VHP, NOAA NESDIS, NOAA/PMEL/NCTR, Royal Society 1888 scan, team-made, NOAA tsunami.gov, weather.gov).
- Team-made diagrams are labelled three times; the 2011 Japan model is labelled as Japan 2011, not as a volcano.
- 2004 is clearly stated as "an earthquake, not a volcano", with a separate card, not mixed with Krakatau imagery; no 2004 footage is shown.
- Kīlauea 4 Jun 2018 footage carries its own date and time of day; Kīlauea clips are never labelled as Tonga or as the tsunami; the Tonga clip is labelled Tonga 15 Jan 2022 each time.
- "Yet the sea rose only about 40 centimeters" avoids the false "no tsunami" premise from the evidence report; "likely" is retained on the earthquake trigger; Krakatau mechanism debate acknowledged; no VEI, no 36,417, no 228,000, no "caldera collapse caused the Tonga tsunami".

- Voice and diagrams disclosed in the credits ("Voice: synthetic (Edge TTS)", "Diagrams: team-made, not to scale").
- Duration 120 s; vertical 1080x1920; no frames with fake or AI footage seen.
